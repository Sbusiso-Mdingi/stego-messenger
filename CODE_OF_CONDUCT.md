# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors should make participation respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Disagreement about methods, results, or security claims should focus on evidence and reproducibility rather than people.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory conduct, threats, publication of private information, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's security/contact channel. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
